FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything about how NurtureTrack works, where your data lives and how to get the most out of it.

Is NurtureTrack really free?

Yes. Every feature is free, there are no ads and no premium plan. The project is supported by optional contributions on Ko-fi.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Open the tracker and start logging straight away.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your browser’s local storage on the device you are using. Nothing is sent to a server, so we never see your baby’s data.

Can my partner and I use it on two phones?

Each device keeps its own data. To move or combine records, open Settings → Backup, download a backup file and import it on the other phone. Importing merges records and skips duplicates.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Clearing site data or browsing history for this site deletes your records. Download a backup regularly — the tracker reminds you when your last backup is more than a week old.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit the tracker works without internet. On iPhone use Share → Add to Home Screen; on Android use the browser menu → Install app.

Which growth standards do you use?

The WHO Child Growth Standards for boys and girls from birth to 5 years. Percentiles are calculated with the official LMS method, the same method used by health professionals.

Is the information on this site medical advice?

No. Our guides summarise public health guidance for general information. Always follow advice from your own doctor, midwife or health visitor.

Which languages are available?

English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese and Japanese. You can switch language at any time; your data is shared across languages on the same device.

How can I support the project?

Share it with other parents, send feedback on GitHub, or buy us a coffee on Ko-fi. Thank you!
