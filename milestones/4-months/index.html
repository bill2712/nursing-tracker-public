4 months

4-month-old baby milestones

A big month for movement: rolling and reaching begin — and sleep may change too.

✨ Skills to look for🌧️ Common challenges🏃 Play & interaction ideas❤️ Care tips ⚠️ Talk to a doctor if

✨ Skills to look for

  • Rolls from tummy to back

    Many babies start rolling, usually from tummy to back first.

  • Reaches and grabs

    Reaches for a toy with one hand and brings it to their mouth.

  • Holds head steady

    Holds their head steady without support when you hold them.

  • Pushes up on hands

    Pushes up onto straight arms during tummy time.

  • Makes sounds back

    Coos and babbles back when you talk to them.

🌧️ Common challenges

  • The “4-month sleep regression”

    Sleep cycles become more adult-like, so a baby who slept well may wake more often for 2–6 weeks. It passes.

  • Distracted feeding

    Babies become so curious that they stop and look around during feeds.

🏃 Play & interaction ideas

  • Floor time

    Give plenty of time on a play mat rather than in bouncers and car seats, with a toy just out of reach.

  • Cross the middle

    Offer toys on one side for the opposite hand to reach — great for coordination.

  • Gentle airplane

    Lift your baby slowly and gently while supporting them well; many love the movement.

❤️ Care tips

  • Stop swaddling

    As soon as your baby shows signs of rolling, stop swaddling with arms in. Switch to a sleeping bag with arms free.

  • Lower the crib

    Lower the crib mattress as your baby becomes more active.

  • Calm feeds

    Feed in a quiet, dim room if your baby is easily distracted. If growth is on track, you do not need to force extra milk.

⚠️ Talk to a doctor if

  • Not holding head steady when held
  • Not cooing or making sounds
  • Not bringing things to their mouth
  • Not pushing down with legs when feet are placed on a firm surface

Every baby develops at their own pace. Milestones are a guide, not a test — most babies reach them within a range of weeks or months. Babies born early often reach milestones based on their corrected age.
