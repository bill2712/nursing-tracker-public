Development

Baby milestones month by month

What to expect from newborn to two years: skills to look for, simple play ideas and care tips for each stage.

  1. 👶 Newborn Newborn milestones Welcome to the world! In the first weeks your baby is adjusting to life outside the womb, learning to feed and getting to know your voice, smell and face. 14 items
  2. 🍼 1 month 1-month-old baby milestones Your baby is awake for a little longer each day, starts to lift their head briefly and loves looking at faces. 10 items
  3. 😊 2 months 2-month-old baby milestones The first real smiles appear! Your baby is more alert, coos back at you and holds their head up better. 10 items
  4. 👐 3 months 3-month-old baby milestones Your baby gains more control of their body, discovers their hands and may laugh out loud for the first time. 9 items
  5. 🔄 4 months 4-month-old baby milestones A big month for movement: rolling and reaching begin — and sleep may change too. 11 items
  6. 🥣 6 months 6-month-old baby milestones Half a year! Many babies are ready for solid food, start to sit with support and babble with repeated sounds. 11 items
  7. 🐾 9 months 9-month-old baby milestones On the move! Crawling, pulling to stand and picking up tiny things — time to babyproof your home. 11 items
  8. 🎂 12 months 12-month (1 year) milestones Happy first birthday! Your baby becomes a toddler: first steps, first words and lots of copying. 11 items
  9. 🏃 18 months 18-month milestones A busy explorer who walks well, climbs everything and is learning new words every week — and the word “no”. 11 items
  10. 🗣️ 24 months 24-month (2 year) milestones Words take off! Two-year-olds join words together, run and jump, and play pretend — while still learning to manage big feelings. 10 items

Every baby develops at their own pace. Milestones are a guide, not a test — most babies reach them within a range of weeks or months. Babies born early often reach milestones based on their corrected age.
