Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

NurtureTrack does not collect, store or share any personal data on servers. Everything you enter stays in your browser on your device.

Data stored on your device

Logs, growth measurements, checklists and settings are saved in your browser’s local storage. They are only readable by this website in your browser.

You can download a backup, import a backup or delete all data at any time in the tracker’s Settings.

Cookies and preferences

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. Your language and theme preferences are saved in local storage so the site remembers them.

Hosting

The site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive or use these logs.

Third-party links

The “Support us” button opens Ko-fi, and the source code link opens GitHub. These sites have their own privacy policies and are only contacted when you click the link.

Children’s data

The tracker is used by parents and carers to record information about their child. Because this information never leaves your device, we never have access to it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Questions? Open an issue on our GitHub repository.
