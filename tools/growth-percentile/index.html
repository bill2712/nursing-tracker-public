WHO growth percentile calculator

Check where your baby’s measurements sit on the WHO Child Growth Standards.

What does the percentile mean?

Out of 100 healthy children of the same age and sex, about 25 would be smaller and the rest larger. Any percentile between the 3rd and 97th is within the normal WHO range.

About the WHO Child Growth Standards

  • The WHO Child Growth Standards describe how healthy, breastfed children grow under good conditions, based on a study of children in Brazil, Ghana, India, Norway, Oman and the United States.
  • Percentiles are calculated with the LMS method (Box-Cox power, median and coefficient of variation), the same method used in clinical growth charts.
  • One measurement is a snapshot. What matters most is that your baby follows a curve over time. A drop or rise across two major percentile lines is worth checking with a professional.

Medical disclaimer

This guide is general information, not medical advice. Every baby is different. If you are worried, contact your doctor, midwife or health visitor. Seek urgent help if your baby has trouble breathing, is unusually sleepy or floppy, or is under 3 months old with a temperature of 38°C (100.4°F) or higher.
